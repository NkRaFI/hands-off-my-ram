# Phase 8 — Progression, balancing, accessibility, and mobile controls

## Objective

Make the endless game approachable, rewarding, and usable across supported input modes.

## Included

- Recovered-RAM progression and selected upgrades
- Easy default balancing
- Recoverable cloud-takeover crisis rather than routine hard reset
- Mobile controls
- Control glyphs from `UI_KENNEY_INPUT_PROMPTS` and MVP controls/panels from `UI_KENNEY_UI_PACK`
- Pause/settings
- Reduced motion/effects option
- Subtitle and volume controls prepared for Phase 9

## Excluded

- Final audio files
- Accounts, cloud saves, multiplayer, monetization

## Acceptance criteria

- New players experience combat and Space Assist quickly.
- The default difficulty does not block major features.
- Controls are usable at desktop and mobile viewport sizes.
- Settings persist locally where appropriate.
- Progression cannot create invalid or negative values.
