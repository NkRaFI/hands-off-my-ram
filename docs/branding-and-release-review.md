# Branding, parody, and release review

This document defines product safeguards, not legal advice. Obtain qualified review before a major commercial release.

## Presentation

- The game is an independent fictional satire.
- Ram Sam, Rocket Elon, and RamAI are fictional caricatures.
- Do not claim or imply endorsement, partnership, certification, or official status.
- Use original project branding in the title, store listing, social previews, and app icon.

## Marks

- Do not include official OpenAI, ChatGPT, SpaceX, X, Tesla, or related logos.
- The RamAI emblem may intentionally evoke an interlocking spiral-flower through RAM-stick geometry, but it must be the project's own asset.
- Do not use the RamAI emblem to impersonate official service UI or authentication.

## Audio

- Do not use altered interview/podcast recordings unless written permission is documented.
- Do not clone or closely imitate real voices.
- Use original fictional performances or commercially permitted non-imitative AI voices.

## Suggested notice

> Hands Off My RAM! is an independent work of parody and fiction. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by OpenAI, SpaceX, their affiliates, or any person parodied in the game. Character voices are fictional performances and are not the real voices of any depicted person.

Final wording should be reviewed before public release.

## Release checklist

- Review all character names, visual likenesses, dialogue, and marketing copy.
- Confirm every third-party asset licence and attribution.
- Confirm voice-provider and music-provider commercial terms.
- Verify that screenshots and trailers contain the parody notice where appropriate.
- Ensure the official title/icon cannot be mistaken for an affiliated product.
- Preserve source and licence evidence for every shipped asset.
