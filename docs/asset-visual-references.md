# Asset visual references

Open these source pages to inspect the exact approved assets before Phase 1. The links are the canonical references used by the asset IDs in `asset-registry.csv`.

## Characters and animations

| Stable ID | What to inspect | Preview/source |
|---|---|---|
| `CHAR_QUATERNIUS_BASE` | Body styles, hairstyles, clothing separation, low-poly proportions, humanoid rig | [Universal Base Characters — Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) |
| `ANIM_QUATERNIUS_CORE` | Idle, walk, run, sprint, reactions, and gestures | [Universal Animation Library — Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) |
| `ANIM_QUATERNIUS_ACTION` | Melee/action clips suitable for the cartoon bat swing | [Universal Animation Library 2 — Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html) |

The player, Ram Sam, Rocket Elon, and NPCs initially use different combinations and recolors from the same character system. This gives the game a cohesive look before custom faces and clothing are created in later polishing.

## City environment

| Stable ID | What to inspect | Preview/source |
|---|---|---|
| `ENV_QUATERNIUS_DOWNTOWN` | Modular buildings, urban props, storefronts, rooftop shapes, and the overall low-poly city style | [Downtown City MegaKit — Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/downtowncitymegakit.html) |

The reference image is a color-and-composition target, not a promise that the unmodified packs look identical. Phase 3 composes and lights these exact packs to approach the vibrant reference.

## Review rule

If you dislike a locked asset after viewing its page, change the decision now and update the catalogue, registry, decision log, and affected phase task together. Once Phase 1 starts, Codex must use the locked IDs and cannot silently choose something else.
